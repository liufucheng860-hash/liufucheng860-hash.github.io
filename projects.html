Optimization-Based Group Testing

MA4254 · Discrete Optimisation

Formulated sparse recovery in non-adaptive group testing as an integer linear program with binary decision variables and test-consistency constraints.

  • Implemented ILP and LP relaxation with randomized rounding, alongside rule-based DNI/DI estimators.
  • Ran simulations with up to 500 individuals under varying testing budgets.
  • Compared exact recovery probability and runtime to investigate accuracy and computational cost.

HDB Resale Price Prediction

ST3131 · Regression Analysis

Analyzed 2,456 resale transactions across 26 Singapore towns from July 2020, modelling log resale prices using property characteristics.

  • Compared a full model with 7 predictor groups against an AIC-selected model retaining 6: floor area, flat age, town, flat type, storey range, and flat model.
  • The final model achieved an in-sample adjusted R² of 0.907 on log prices, reproduced from the archived data and model specification.
  • Examined residual diagnostics and interpreted quantitative and categorical effects. The reported fit is in-sample; the archive does not contain a held-out test evaluation.

DNA-Binding Protein Classification

IT1244 · Artificial Intelligence: Technology and Impact

Worked in a 3-person team to distinguish DNA-binding from non-binding proteins using 53,285 training sequences and 13,452 test sequences.

  • Compared 3 model families: XGBoost, convolutional neural networks, and Random Forest.
  • The team presentation reported a Random Forest test MCC of 0.4095, sensitivity of 61.50%, and specificity of 79.68%.
  • Contributed to code revision and execution, team coordination, literature review, report development, and presentation preparation.

Performance figures are from the original team presentation; sample counts were checked against the archived FASTA files.
