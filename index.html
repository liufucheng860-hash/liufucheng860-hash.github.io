Hello! I am an undergraduate at the National University of Singapore, majoring in Mathematics, with a second major in Quantitative Finance and a minor in Data Science and Analytics. I expect to graduate in May 2027.

My interests include financial time series, multimodal learning, and optimization. My recent research investigates how price features, policy information, and corporate announcements can be combined for agricultural stock movement prediction.

I have worked on factor replication, backtesting, and event studies during quantitative research internships at China Merchants Securities and Avalok Capital. These experiences have motivated me to strengthen my mathematical foundations and study the reliability of data-driven methods in finance.

I am exploring PhD opportunities in related areas. Please see my Research, Projects, and CV for more details. You are welcome to contact me about research opportunities or shared interests.
