Research

I am interested in the use of mathematical and statistical methods for financial prediction and decision-making, particularly when the relevance of information changes over time.

Manuscripts under review

Adaptive Temporal-Context Fusion for Policy-Aware Multimodal Agricultural Stock Movement Prediction

Fucheng Liu, Chenxue Yang

Under review at Expert Systems with Applications, 2026.

[Request manuscript] [Summary]

Summary

This study investigates the combination of historical price features, macroeconomic policy information, and corporate announcements for agricultural stock movement prediction. The proposed temporal-context fusion approach examines how contextual information can be incorporated into a multimodal prediction model.

A copy is available on request.

Research Experience

Policy-Aware Multimodal Stock Movement Prediction

First author · Research with Chenxue Yang

  • Research design: Developed the research question and methodology for studying how the relevance of policy information and corporate announcements changes over time in agricultural stock prediction.
  • Implementation: Implemented and debugged the Python research pipeline for data processing, model training, and evaluation, including the adaptive temporal-context fusion of market, policy, and announcement information.
  • Experiments and analysis: Contributed to comparative experiments and ablation analysis. The study evaluates 5-, 10-, and 20-day prediction horizons with 5 random seeds, using 4 development stocks and 4 additional stocks for external validation.
  • Scientific writing: Wrote the initial manuscript draft, documented the methodology and experimental findings, and revised the manuscript in response to feedback from my co-author.

Related manuscript: Adaptive Temporal-Context Fusion for Policy-Aware Multimodal Agricultural Stock Movement Prediction.
